About Analize Statistice Research Foundation

“A scientific and scholastic body devoted to the advancement of empirical, statistical, and multidisciplinary research.”

Who We Are

Analize Statistice Research Foundation (ASRF), based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, can be positioned as a multidisciplinary research and knowledge-development institution dedicated to generating reliable evidence, advancing scientific inquiry, and translating research findings into practical solutions for social development, public policy, institutional improvement, and community well-being.

Through peer-reviewed open access dissemination, Regional/National /International conference, and hands-on training workshops, ASRF actively bridges academic research with public policy and industrial innovation.

👁️

Vision

To become a credible and collaborative research institution recognised for methodological excellence, statistical innovation, interdisciplinary scholarship, and meaningful contributions to evidence-based social development.

🎯

Mission

To produce and disseminate reliable research evidence through scientific inquiry, advanced statistical analysis, collaborative research, and capacity building, thereby supporting informed decisions and practical solutions to societal challenges.

President's Address

Analize Statistice Research Foundation (ASRF)

Renjith R.S. • Chief Research Investigator, Analize Statistice Research Foundation | Editor-in-Chief, ASRF Journal of Evidence, Voices & Action (EVA)

Welcome to the Analize Statistice Research Foundation (ASRF), a scientific and scholastic body devoted to the advancement of empirical, statistical, and multidisciplinary research.

At ASRF, we believe that scientific inquiry and reliable evidence are essential for advancing knowledge, informing policy, and addressing contemporary societal challenges. Our mission is to promote rigorous research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and ethical scholarly practices that contribute to academic excellence and social development.

Through our research initiatives and the ASRF Journal of Evidence, Voices & Action (EVA), we aspire to create meaningful opportunities for knowledge generation, scholarly dialogue, and evidence-based action.

I warmly invite researchers, academicians, and institutions to join us in advancing scientific knowledge and translating research into meaningful societal progress.

With scholarly regards,

Renjith R.S.
