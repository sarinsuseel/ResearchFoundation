Aim & Scope of the Journal

The ASRF Journal of Applied Statistics & Multidisciplinary Research provides an international forum for the publication of original research papers, reviews, and short communications in the fields of statistical methods, data science, behavioral analytics, economics, and applied multidisciplinary sciences.

We particularly welcome research bridging theoretical statistical modeling with real-world problems in public health, industrial optimization, econometrics, computational intelligence, and social sciences.

Author Guidelines & Manuscript Formatting

Authors wishing to submit manuscripts to ASRF Journal should ensure their draft adheres strictly to our standard academic preparation guidelines:

1. Manuscript Structure

Articles must include Title, Author Names & Affiliations, Corresponding Email, Abstract (max 250 words), 4–6 Keywords, Introduction, Methodology, Results & Statistical Findings, Discussion, Conclusion, and References.

2. Referencing & Citation Style

The journal adopts APA (7th Edition) or IEEE citation format. All cited works must include verified DOI links wherever available.

3. Plagiarism & Originality Benchmark

Manuscripts must not exceed 10% similarity index excluding bibliography. Plagiarized submissions will be summarily rejected.

💡 Submission Inquiries: Inquiries regarding prospective submissions or special issues may be addressed to our editorial email at editorial@asrf-foundation.org.

Double-Blind Peer Review Policy

To safeguard rigorous academic impartiality, the ASRF Journal operates a Double-Blind Peer Review model. Neither the authors nor the reviewers are aware of each other's identities throughout the evaluation phase.

Phase 1
Editorial Scope & Plagiarism Triage
Phase 2
2 Independent Reviewers Assigned
Phase 3
Author Revision & Statistical Audit
Phase 4
Final Acceptance & DOI Assignment

Turnaround timeline from initial evaluation to peer review decision averages 3 to 6 weeks, maintaining high publication integrity.

Publication Ethics & Malpractice Statement

ASRF follows standard guidelines formulated by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE). We maintain zero tolerance for academic fabrication, data falsification, duplicate submission, or improper authorship claims.

  • Authors must ensure original research and declare all competing financial or personal interests.
  • Reviewers must treat manuscripts confidentially and recuse themselves in instances of conflict of interest.
  • Editors retain final authority regarding acceptance, rejection, or post-publication corrections.
