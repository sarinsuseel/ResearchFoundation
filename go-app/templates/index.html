Aim and Scope of Analize Statistice Research Foundation (ASRF)

Analize Statistice Research Foundation (ASRF), based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, can be positioned as a multidisciplinary research and knowledge-development institution dedicated to generating reliable evidence, advancing scientific inquiry, and translating research findings into practical solutions for social development, public policy, institutional improvement, and community well-being.

How the Editorial Review & Publication Workflow Operates

Transparent, standard academic peer-review lifecycle from email submission to publication:

1
Email Paper

Author emails Word or PDF manuscript with Title, Author details, and Abstract.

2
Initial Screening

Chief editor audits scope, originality, and formatting benchmarks within 24–48 hours.

3
Peer Review

Assigned to 2 independent academic reviewers for rigorous double-blind evaluation.

4
Email Acceptance & Live DOI

Decision letter emailed to author. Upon acceptance, the article is published live with Open Access!
