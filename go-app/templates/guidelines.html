1. Manuscript Structure & Format

Manuscripts must be submitted in Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx) or PDF (.pdf) format. All submissions should follow standard IMRAD academic structuring:

  • Title: Concise, informative (10–25 words). Avoid formulaic acronyms.
  • Abstract: Self-contained summary between 150 and 350 words detailing background, methodology, results, and conclusion.
  • Keywords: 3 to 6 indexable terms separated by commas.
  • Introduction: Contextualize the research problem and literature review.
  • Methodology: Explicit detail ensuring reproducibility.
  • Results & Discussion: Empirical data, tables, figures, and comparative interpretation.
  • References: Standard APA or IEEE format.

2. Editorial Peer-Review Protocol

ASRF operates a Double-Blind Peer Review process. The reviewer does not know the author's identity, and vice versa.

Upon submission, an immediate notification is dispatched to the Editorial Committee. Initial editorial triage (plagiarism & scope check) is performed within 24 hours, followed by specialist evaluation within 48 to 72 hours.

3. Plagiarism & Ethical Standards

All manuscripts undergo automated similarity screening. Papers showing overall similarity exceeding 10% (excluding bibliography) are rejected or returned for revision. Authors must ensure appropriate citation of prior research.
